## README.md — Relevant Excerpts

> The full README is at [`README.md`](../README.md). Key sections relevant to app ideas:

### Project Description

> FSA Elite is built for salespeople who want **more** — more confidence, more skill, more production,
> and more results. From training to branding, FSA Elite helps closers level up their mindset, master
> the process, and stand out in a competitive world.
>
> Founded by **Kaygun Fontenot** — 25× consecutive Sales of the Month, 23 years old,
> car-industry veteran, LLC registered — FSA Elite is the training program Kaygun always wished existed.

### AI Roleplay Feature

> FSA Elite includes a built-in AI roleplay trainer at `/roleplay`. It simulates a tough, skeptical
> car-buying customer powered by **OpenAI GPT-4o mini**, so you can practice:
>
> - Handling live objections (price, payment, "I need to think about it", competitor comparisons)
> - Building rapport and trust
> - Guiding customers through the decision process without pressure

### Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (pages-based), TypeScript
- **Payments:** Stripe (checkout sessions + planned webhooks)
- **AI:** OpenAI GPT-4o mini (AI roleplay & coaching)
- **Auth (planned):** Supabase or Firebase
- **Deployment:** Vercel (recommended) or GitHub Pages (static fallback)
- **Domain:** `fsaeliteperformance.com` (store) · `fsaelite.org` (training, separate project)
